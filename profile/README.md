## What I build

- **Business automation:** Built a local-model finance agent in an afternoon. It has operated for two months between Flowlogic and Xero, processing approximately 175 invoices and $107,860 of invoicing each week while saving two hours of daily administration.
- **Governed agents:** Build permission controls, approval gates, audit records and deterministic checks around systems that act on real applications.
- **Private AI infrastructure:** Built a four-node AI and home-network environment from the ground up in four weeks, working solo, with virtualisation, containers, private GitLab and CI, model serving and monitoring.
- **Model engineering:** Customise, evaluate and deploy local models with measured serving configurations and explicit quality checks.
